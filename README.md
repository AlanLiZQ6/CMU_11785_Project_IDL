# CMU_11785_Project_IDL

**Abstract**:

Accurate and rapid flow-field prediction is crucial in many engineering applications, yet traditional Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) solvers often require extensive computational time, particularly for complex geometries. In scenarios like real-time analysis and design optimization, for example, quick access to reliable flow data is essential. Surrogate modeling offers a promising alternative by approximating CFD results with significantly reduced computational cost. Our project proposes developing and testing a graph convolutional network (GCN)-based surrogate model that predicts steady, incompressible 2D flow fields around simple obstacles (such as circles or ellipses). By comparing the accuracy and computational speed of our GCN surrogate with standard CFD results, we aim to demonstrate that our approach can deliver fast, reliable predictions, thereby offering valuable benefits for a wide range of engineering challenges.
